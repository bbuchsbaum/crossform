# Matched population interpretability recovery court

Status: normative matched-simulation contract

Version: population-interpretability-v1

Date: 2026-08-21

This hierarchical court lifts the three certified planted organizations from
the matched-interpretability court into a 24-subject population model. Across
200 paired replications, the same subject covariate, magnitude variation,
transport quality, organization jitter, component noise, and planted
influential subject are reused across the broad/coherent, mixed, and
fine/configurational scenarios. Their scale totals remain matched.

The court fits total, coherent, and configuration coefficients directly.
Coherent shares are derived only after population coefficients are estimated;
participant ratios are never averaged. It records intercept and covariate
recovery, HC3 pointwise conditional coverage with Monte Carlo error,
coefficient conservation, scale-profile correlation, leave-one-subject
influence, coverage, subject-set identity, and transport quality.

The supported arm has complete coverage and remains conditional on realized
transport. The failure arm selects participants using their latent coherent
response and covariate. It is retained to measure the conditional-versus-
marginal target shift and is explicitly ineligible for a marginal claim.

Prespecified gates require strict broad greater than mixed greater than fine
coherent-share ordering at every nonpoint scale, total coefficient spread at
most 0.08, conservation at 1e-10, supported HC3 conditional coverage two-MCSE
lower bound at least 0.88, scale-profile correlation at least 0.98, recovery of
the planted influential subject in at least 65 percent of paired runs, and
informative target shift at least 0.05. These are synthetic-regime claims, not
empirical validation.
