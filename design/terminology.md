# Crossform terminology contract

Status: normative public-language contract

Version: crossform-terminology-v1

| Preferred prose | API token | Meaning | Deprecated shorthand | Forbidden interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| crossvalidated bilinear geometry | geometry plan | Fixed linear geometry under declared independent pairings | common pipeline | universal theory of every RSA method |
| total effect component | total | Reproducible squared effect under the declared metric/frame | energy result without units | activation mechanism |
| coherent component | coherent | Frame-weighted local mean-mode contribution to the estimand | coherent energy when the component role is unclear | coherent neural mechanism or coherent brain process |
| configurational component | configuration | Reproducible spatial-pattern remainder beyond that mean mode | configuration energy | configurational neural mechanism or configuration network |
| coherent share | coherence_fraction | Ratio derived from aggregated components only when its signed denominator and nonnegative partition are admitted | average coherence | average of participant ratios |
| point scale | radius-0 | Featurewise member of a scale family | zero-radius searchlight | absence of spatial structure |
| radius N units | radius-N | Neighborhood scale with coordinate units stated as mm, voxels, or features | scale N | a biologically intrinsic scale |
| detection map | local normalization | Intensive per-neighborhood evidence used to locate effects | importance map | additive territory budget |
| attribution ledger | conservative normalization | Extensive allocation of one total across nodes | detection map | thresholded activation map |
| population coefficient | model term | Coefficient for the declared coverage target conditional on realized transport | group effect | marginal population truth without its conditioning |

Nouns and adjectives are deliberate: the stored component is
configuration; configurational modifies component, contribution, or profile.
Coherent and configurational are mathematical estimand components. They are not
ontological categories of regions, subjects, or neural mechanisms.

Detection asks where evidence is locally strong. Attribution asks how one
declared total is allocated over a conservative frame. Neither licenses the
other reading. Descriptive estimates, spectra, influence, and support
diagnostics use describe, attribute, or associate. Detect, establish, infer,
and replicate are reserved for procedures whose uncertainty and evidence class
support those verbs.

Every scale label states point or radius plus coordinate units. Every effect
axis states signed response units or squared response units; a dimensionless
share is labelled as such. Population plots attach subject-set and coverage
metadata rather than implying that identical coordinates guarantee identical
contributors.
